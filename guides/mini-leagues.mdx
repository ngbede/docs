## Overview
Mini leagues let users compete in smaller groups while sharing the same fantasy league. Create them after your fantasy league is set up.

## Key endpoints
- [Create mini league](/api-reference/mini-league/create)
- [List mini leagues](/api-reference/mini-league/get-list)
- [Get mini league](/api-reference/mini-league/get-by-id)

## Typical flow

<Steps>
<Step title="Create a mini league">
Create a mini league and associate it with a `fantasy_league_id`.
</Step>

<Step title="List and retrieve">
List mini leagues for discovery, and fetch details when users open a specific league.
</Step>
</Steps>

## Overview
Player prices define the valuation of players in your fantasy league. Upload prices and list them as needed.

## Key endpoints
- [Upload player prices](/api-reference/price/upload)
- [List player prices](/api-reference/price/get-list)

## Typical flow

<Steps>
<Step title="Upload prices">
Upload a pricing payload for the league. Store the reference in your system if you need to track versions.
</Step>

<Step title="List prices">
Retrieve prices to display in your client or sync downstream systems.
</Step>
</Steps>

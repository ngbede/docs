## Overview
Points determine how events are scored in your fantasy league. Upload a configuration per league and retrieve it when needed.

## Key endpoints
- [Upload points config](/api-reference/points/upload-config)
- [Get points config](/api-reference/points/get-config)
- [Get points events](/api-reference/points/get-events)

## Typical flow

<Steps>
<Step title="Upload configuration">
Upload a points configuration for your fantasy league.
</Step>

<Step title="Retrieve configuration">
Fetch the stored configuration whenever you need to render scoring rules.
</Step>

<Step title="Read event scoring">
Use the points events endpoint to display scoring outcomes for fixtures.
</Step>
</Steps>

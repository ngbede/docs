## Overview
Fantasy leagues are the foundation for mini leagues, squads, points, and pricing. Start here and store the `fantasy_league_id` for subsequent requests.

## Key endpoints
- [Create fantasy league](/api-reference/fantasy-league/create)
- [List fantasy leagues](/api-reference/fantasy-league/get-list)
- [Get fantasy league](/api-reference/fantasy-league/get-by-id)
- [Update fantasy league](/api-reference/fantasy-league/update)
- [Get league clubs](/api-reference/fantasy-league/clubs)
- [Get league gameweeks](/api-reference/fantasy-league/gameweeks)
- [Get league fixtures](/api-reference/fantasy-league/fixtures)

## Typical flow

<Steps>
<Step title="Create the league">
Create a new fantasy league and capture the `fantasy_league_id` from the response.
</Step>

<Step title="Load season data">
Fetch clubs, gameweeks, and fixtures to populate your client experience and to align with your season timeline.
</Step>

<Step title="Keep details current">
Use the update endpoint if the league configuration changes. Re-sync any cached league data as needed.
</Step>
</Steps>

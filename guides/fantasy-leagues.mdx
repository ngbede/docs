## Overview
Fantasy leagues are the foundation for mini leagues, squads, and scoring. Start by creating a league, then load clubs, gameweeks, and fixtures as needed.

## Key endpoints
- [Create fantasy league](/api-reference/fantasy-league/create)
- [List fantasy leagues](/api-reference/fantasy-league/get-list)
- [Get fantasy league](/api-reference/fantasy-league/get-by-id)
- [Update fantasy league](/api-reference/fantasy-league/update)
- [Get league clubs](/api-reference/fantasy-league/clubs)
- [Get league gameweeks](/api-reference/fantasy-league/gameweeks)
- [Get league fixtures](/api-reference/fantasy-league/fixtures)

## Typical flow

<Steps>
<Step title="Create the league">
Create a new fantasy league to act as the parent for mini leagues, squads, points, and prices.
</Step>

<Step title="Fetch league data">
Pull clubs, gameweeks, and fixtures for the league to populate your client experience.
</Step>

<Step title="Iterate and update">
If your league configuration changes, update it and re-fetch the data you display to users.
</Step>
</Steps>
